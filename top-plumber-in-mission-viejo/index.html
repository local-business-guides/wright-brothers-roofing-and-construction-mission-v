What roofing work covers

The right roofing service depends on the condition and location of the damage. Shingle replacement means installing new shingles where needed. Structural repair addresses the roof's stability, while storm damage repair is for restoring a roof after storm damage. Emergency hail damage repair addresses roof impairments caused by hail.

Before contacting a contractor, consider this short checklist:

  • Write down the room or roof area where you noticed a problem.
  • Note when you first saw it and whether it has changed.
  • Take pictures from a safe place, without climbing or reaching onto the roof.
  • Keep any relevant details about recent damage or repairs handy.

Avoid patching a roof without understanding the source of the problem. A quick cover-up may not address damage underneath, and walking on the roof can create a safety risk. Call a roofing professional when you see damaged shingles, signs of roof impact, or concerns about the roof's structure.
