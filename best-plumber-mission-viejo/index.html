What to know before calling a roofer

It helps to gather a few details before you call. Note where you noticed the issue, what it looks like, and when you first saw it. If you have pictures taken from a safe spot, keep them handy. A simple homeowner checklist

Before you contact a contractor, take a moment to sort out what you know. This can make the first conversation more useful without requiring you to diagnose the problem yourself.

  • Is the issue at a sink, toilet, drain, or pipe, or does it involve the roof?
  • Where inside or outside the home did you notice it?
  • When did you first see it, and have you noticed any change?
  • Can you share a photo taken without climbing onto the roof?

One mistake to avoid is calling a roofer for a plumbing repair, or calling a plumber to replace damaged shingles. Another is trying a roof patch before the source of the damage is understood. If you see a roof concern, keep people away from the affected area and contact a roofing contractor. If the signs point to a pipe or fixture, contact a plumbing contractor.
